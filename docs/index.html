What is the Public Good Data Wrangle?

Brought to you by the Martin Institute for Public Good and the Davidson College Department of Data Science, the Public Good Data Wrangle (PGDW) is an annual one-day “data science hackathon” that brings together a group of interdisciplinary student programmers and data enthusiasts to tackle a challenge that relates to issues of public significance.

In 2025, we analyzed millions of rows of high school and college admission data to look for potential inequities in the college recruitment process.

In 2026… the sky is the limit!

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

The Public Good Data Wrangle is open to all currently-enrolled Davidson students. We want a diverse group representing various majors, class years, and skill levels to participate!
Participation is 100% free to Davidson students! The college will provide meals, snacks, and drinks for all participants.
Bring your laptop and charger, a water bottle, and any other essentials you might need for a full day of building cool stuff with data!
While the Data Wrangle does organize students into teams of three, we do not require you to bring your own team with you. If you show up solo, we will find a team for you! But you can also form your own team prior to DataFest if you like by adding your teammates’ names on the registration form.
